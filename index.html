Frequently Asked Questions

Can I combine different services for a custom package?

Absolutely! We encourage custom packages that address your specific needs. Contact us to discuss your requirements, and we'll create a tailored solution with preferred pricing for combined services.

What determines the price range for services?

Our pricing varies based on the complexity, scale, and requirements of your project. Factors include the number of pages for websites, frequency and type of content for social media, campaign scope for digital marketing, and design complexity for branding projects.

What are the payment terms?

We typically require a 50% upfront payment to begin work, with the remaining balance due upon completion. For ongoing services like SEO or Social Media Management, we bill monthly. All payments can be made via bank transfer, credit card, or digital payment methods.

How long does it take to complete a project?

Project timelines vary by service: a basic website typically takes 2-3 weeks, while more complex web applications may take 6-8 weeks. Branding projects average 1-2 weeks, and SEO results typically become visible within 2-3 months. We'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.
